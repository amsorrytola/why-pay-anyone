# Data sources

Registry of every dataset. Round 1 audit: 2026-11-21 (page reads and HTTP checks). Round 2: 2026-11-22 (files downloaded and opened). Raw files are in `data/raw/<source>/` (not committed); `data/manifest.json` records the URL, size, SHA-256 and retrieval time of each one, and `pipeline/fetch.py` and `pipeline/fetch_litellm_history.py` re-download them. Licences are as stated on the source pages, not legal advice.

Status: **VERIFIED** (downloaded and opened, contents confirmed) · **PARTLY** (exists, not fully checked) · **UNVERIFIED** (not checked) · **EXCLUDED** (not used).

| Source | Where | Licence | Used in | Status and what the files contain |

| Anthropic Economic Index, release 2026-06-26 | Hugging Face `Anthropic/EconomicIndex` | CC-BY | M2, M4, India | **VERIFIED.** `aei_claude_ai` (219 MB, 1,636,573 rows): Claude.ai usage by country (121 countries including India), subregion (652, including Indian states) and global, in categories `overall`, `soc_occupation`, `onet` (work activities) and `request`. **Only two monthly windows: April and May 2026**, so no long time series. Country-level `usage_per_capita_index` exists (India 0.31 in April, 0.30 in May 2026; US 4.25 and 3.87). Metrics also include automation vs augmentation shares, AI autonomy, human-only time and time with AI. `aei_1p_api` (77 MB, 491,705 rows): API usage, global only, no India. Claude Code is excluded from both. Covers Anthropic's products only |

| US Census BTOS | census.gov/hfp/btos | US government data | M3 | **VERIFIED.** Seven Excel files (National, Sector, Subsector, Employment Size Class, State, Top 25 MSA, URR), 82 fortnightly periods, 2023 to 2026-23. AI questions: Q7 firm uses AI in any function (26 periods, 2025-24 to 2026-23; 25.0% yes in the latest), Q8 employees use generative AI (2 periods), Q9 effect of AI on headcount and Q10 effect on hiring (2 periods only), Q28 expected use. In the files the question is worded "Super Intelligence or SI (formerly referred to as Artificial Intelligence or AI)". So Q7 is a one-year series; Q9/Q10 are a snapshot, not a series. Earlier AI wording is not in these files |
| Epoch AI datasets | epoch.ai/data | CC BY | M1, M5, M6 | **VERIFIED** (5 zips, chat stats workbook, polling CSV). `benchmark_data.zip`: about 90 benchmark files, `epoch_capabilities_index/eci_scores.csv` (Epoch Capabilities Index with confidence intervals, 258 models), `model_metadata.csv` (1,276 models), `gdpval_external.csv`, `metr_time_horizons_external.csv`, and several files with a cost per task. `ai_models.zip`: 3,730 models with training compute. `ai_companies.zip`: funding, revenue, staff, usage and compute-spend reports (revenue reports: 62 with numbers, nine companies, mostly Anthropic and OpenAI, with large gaps, so too thin for market-share calculations). `ai_chip_sales.zip` and `data_centers.zip`: chips and data centres over time. | OpenRouter models API | `openrouter.ai/api/v1/models` | OpenRouter terms | M1 | **VERIFIED** for today's prices (476 models, snapshot dated in the manifest) |
| OpenRouter rankings-daily | OpenRouter datasets API | CC BY 4.0 with citation | M1, M5 | **VERIFIED.** Fetched with the project's OpenRouter API key by `pipeline/fetch_openrouter_rankings.py`: 35,088 rows, 688 days (2025-01-01 to 2026-11-21), 51 rows per day (the top 50 public models plus an "other" row); two days are missing from the source (2025-06-15 and 2025-07-15). Token totals are prompt plus completion tokens as counted by each provider's own tokenizer, so they are not strictly comparable across providers. The top 50 models make up about 94% of all tokens. Reported volume grows from about 72 billion tokens a day in January 2025 to about 35 trillion a day in November 2026 (as reported; check before relying on it). Covers OpenRouter's own users only (developers and apps that route through it). Citation to carry: "Source: OpenRouter (openrouter.ai/rankings), as of 2026-11-22. Licensed under CC BY 4.0." |
| LLM price history | LiteLLM `model_prices_and_context_window.json` git history (BerriAI/litellm) | LiteLLM licence unclear (NOASSERTION) | M1 | **VERIFIED as a source.** 39 month-end snapshots, 2023-09 to 2026-11 (72 to 7,099 entries, about 29 MB). | India PLFS microdata | microdata.gov.in | Government of India, data-access agreement | India Stage B | PARTLY.
